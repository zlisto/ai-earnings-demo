Tesla Q4 2024 Earnings Presentation

2025-01-29

Business Update

Q4 2024 was a record quarter for both vehicle deliveries and energy storage deployments. Model Y is expected to be the best-selling vehicle globally for the full year 2024. Tesla has also made significant investments in infrastructure for future growth, including manufacturing capabilities for new models, AI training compute, and energy storage capacity.

Key Highlights:
  • Lowest cost per vehicle ever at <$35,000, driven largely by raw material cost improvement
  • New Model Y now launched in all markets
  • FSD (Supervised) continues to rapidly improve with aim of exceeding human levels of safety
  • Robotaxi business expected to begin launching later this year in parts of the U.S.

Energy

Tesla's Energy business achieved record deployments for both Powerwall and Megapack at a combined 11.0 GWh in Q4, resulting in a record gross profit. Energy storage deployments for the full year 2024 reached 31.4 GWh, a 114% increase year-over-year.

Q4 Energy Storage Deployment: 11.0 GWh
2024 Energy Storage Deployment: 31.4 GWh, 114% increase Y/Y
Key Energy Highlights
  • Material and other costs continued to come down in Q4 at the Lathrop Megafactory
  • Both Powerwall and Megapack continue to be supply constrained as Tesla opens new markets
  • Construction of Megafactory Shanghai was completed in December
  • Shanghai Megafactory will begin ramping in Q1 2025
  • Energy storage deployments expected to grow at least 50% year-over-year in 2025
