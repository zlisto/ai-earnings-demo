Subscriber Growth

Net subscriber additions of approximately 19 million in 2024, with strong growth in the advertising tier.

Ad Plan Growth: Ad-supported plan represented over 55% of sign-ups across ad-enabled countries in Q4 2024
Ad Membership Increase: 30% quarter-over-quarter growth in Q4, following 35% growth in Q3
Engagement: View hours per member on the ad plan is similar to engagement on the standard non-ads plan
Revenue Outlook: Ad revenue doubled year-over-year in 2024 and expected to double again in 2025
